# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-09-22

ShushDJX is free and open-source software. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data.

## Data Collection

The app does not collect, transmit, or share user data.
The app also does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or
third-party services that collect user data.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Because the app does not collect or transmit user data, the developer does not retain any user data and has no user data to delete.

## Third Parties

The app does not share user data with third parties (because it has none to share).

## Permissions Used

* `android.permission.BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE` – required to be notified of media track changes, which is what the app uses to recognize DJ X narration tracks and trigger the "skip track" functionality that is the point of this app.

No other permissions are requested or used.

Changes to this privacy policy will be published at this location.
